title: Safety and sharing
description: What matters more than getting the shot.
---

## Look around before the screen

- Stay away from roads, tracks, cliffs, and water edges.
- Do not keep watching the screen while walking.
- Do not look for dark shooting locations alone.
- Never leave a phone where it can fall or block a path.

## Protect people and information

- Ask before photographing a person, and ask again before sharing.
- Check for uniforms, addresses, location data, or private details on documents.
- Do not immediately post a picture that reveals your current location to strangers.

If a situation feels unsafe, stop shooting and talk to a trusted adult.
