---
title: Ten-minute challenges
description: Change one thing and notice what happens.
---

## 1. Three zoom choices

From the same safe position, make one picture at 0.5×, 1×, and 2×. Compare what happens near the edges.

## 2. Light direction

Photograph one object with a window in front, beside, and behind it. Find how shadows reveal shape.

## 3. Still versus moving

From a safe distance, compare Photo with the observed Pro starting point of 1/350.

## 4. Collect three colors

Find three objects with the same color. Avoid capturing faces or private details without permission.
