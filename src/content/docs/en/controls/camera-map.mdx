## Places to remember

1. **0.5× / 1× / 2×**: field-of-view choices. Whether 2× is a separate telephoto lens remains unconfirmed.
2. **White circle**: the shutter button.
3. **Photo, Portrait, Details**: where you choose a mode.
4. **Turning-arrow icon**: switches between front and rear cameras.

**Pro** is in the bottom mode strip, not in the Details tile grid.

Screen labels can change after a camera-app update.
