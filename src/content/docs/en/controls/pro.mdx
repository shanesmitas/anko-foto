## Start from Auto

The Pro screen shows ISO, shutter speed (S), white balance (WB), and focus (AF). Leave most controls on Auto and change just one, so you can see what it does.

| Control | What it changes            | Observed on this device |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| S       | Freezes or trails movement | Auto, 1/350             |
| ISO     | Brightness and grain       | Auto, 100, 200          |
| WB      | Color                      | Auto                    |
| AF      | Focus                      | Auto                    |

### Starting point for motion

Set S to **1/350** and leave ISO, WB, and AF on **Auto**. If motion still blurs, move the shutter faster without assuming a target endpoint. The full ranges remain unknown.

## What changes?

- Faster shutter: less light and a darker image, but less motion blur.
- Slower shutter: more light and a brighter image, but more motion blur and camera-shake risk.
- Higher ISO: a brighter image with more noise.
- Lower ISO: a cleaner but darker image unless you add light or use a slower shutter.
- EV adjusts automatic exposure. It may not remain available when shutter and ISO are both manual.
- Aperture is absent from the observed control row, so this guide does not teach it as an adjustable setting.
