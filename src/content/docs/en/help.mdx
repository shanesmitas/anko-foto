---
title: Help when a photo goes wrong
description: Quick fixes for blur, darkness, focus, and color.
---

## It is blurry

- Use both hands and gently tuck in your elbows.
- Move toward brighter light.
- In Pro for motion, start at 1/350 and move the shutter faster if needed.

## It is too dark

- Move the subject near a window or streetlight.
- At night, try Night Vision and stay still just after shooting.
- Avoid startling people or animals with flash.

## Focus will not lock

- Tap the part that should be sharp.
- If you are extremely close, step back slightly.
- Gently clean the lens with a soft cloth.

## The color looks wrong

- Check whether different kinds of lights are mixing.
- In Pro, return WB to Auto.
