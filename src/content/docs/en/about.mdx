This guide treats the supplied physical-device screenshots as its strongest evidence. It publishes an exact recipe value only when that value was observed on the device, or when the commercial device identity has been confirmed and official information for that exact model verifies it.

## Device name status

The provisional official baseline is **motorola moto g66j 5G**, but the exact commercial identity of the photographed device remains unconfirmed. g66j-only specifications can be discussed here with a provisional label, but cannot validate exact recipe settings. Changing a display label alone never promotes the identity to verified.

## Three high-priority requested captures

1. About phone showing the commercial product name and Android version.
2. Pro at 1× with shutter moved to the slowest manual value—not Auto (A).
3. Pro at 0.5× with shutter moved to the slowest manual value—not Auto (A).

## Other documented unknowns

- Android, Camera app, and security-update versions.
- Whether 2× is a distinct telephoto lens or a crop.
- Complete video resolutions/fps, stabilization, and external-microphone support.
- Slow-motion fps, timelapse speeds, and complete photo-resolution options.
- Detailed HDR, RAW, location, grid, and storage behavior.
- The opened RAW selector, ISO/WB endpoints, EV step size, fastest shutter, and 0.5× Pro behavior.
