---
title: Mode quick guide
description: Observed camera modes and when to use each one.
---

| Mode           | Good for               | First tip                       |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Photo          | Everyday pictures      | Tap the subject at 1×           |
| Portrait       | People and pets        | Add distance behind the subject |
| Night Vision   | Dark streets or rooms  | Hold with two hands             |
| Panorama       | Long views             | Sweep slowly and evenly         |
| Maximum Pixels | Bright, still detail   | Keep the phone steady           |
| Pro            | Adjusting motion       | Change one control at a time    |
| Scan           | Paper and notes        | Hold parallel to the page       |
| Timelapse      | Clouds and shadows     | Secure the phone safely         |
| Slow motion    | Short actions          | Use brighter light              |
| Tilt shift     | Miniature-style scenes | Find a view with many details   |

Confirm unknown values such as Slow motion frame rates on the device itself.
